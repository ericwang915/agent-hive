One Queen. Many bees.
Your coding agents, working as a hive.

A free, open-source Mac app that turns Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Cursor Agent into a small, friendly team you can watch, schedule and steer.

Squads

Drag bees into a team

Drag a bee onto the bee it should follow. Drop two on the same one and they work in parallel; drag a bee onto a second one and it waits for both and merges their work.

A reviewer can send its findings back to the writer until it approves, for at most three rounds.

Queen

A lead agent instead of ten terminals

Tell Queen what you want. She picks the bees, hands out the work, waits for the results and sums them up. She only wakes the bees she needs.

Skins

Bees by default. Maids or butlers if you like.

Settings → Appearance changes who your agents are drawn as. Every bee keeps its own colour, has seven faces, and moves while it works, idles, sleeps or hits an error.

Maid caféAnime maids, one hair colour per bee.
Butler installAnime butlers in tailcoats. One click in More skins.

Honey bee, Maid café and the classic bee Agent Hive started with come with the app. Other skins are downloaded only when you install them, and more are on the way.

🐝Any agent, mixedClaude Code writes, Codex reviews, Gemini tests. Any CLI that takes a prompt can be a bee.
🌙Works while you are awaySchedules and event triggers, catch-up after sleep, and a daily budget for automatic runs.

Install

Up and buzzing in a minute

One line in Terminal
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ericwang915/agent-hive/main/install.sh | bash
Or with Homebrew
brew install --cask ericwang915/tap/agent-hive
Or download the .dmg. You need at least one agent CLI installed, such as claude, codex or gemini.
